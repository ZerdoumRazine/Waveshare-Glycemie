## No actual health data

The catches were taken in **demonstration mode** (`demo normal`), which replaces any
the 24-h history by a **synthetic** curve generated by the firmware. This is for
that the red bandeau **MODE DEMO** is visible at the top of each image: the value
displayed, the curve and all statistics on the page AUJOURD'HUI are **fictitious**.

The demo mode does not write anything about the microSD and does not change any persistent data.

## A hidden value in `06_reglages_langue.png`

True capture of 17 October 2026. The local IP address of the device is covered with a banner
grey, as the apparatus does itself for the dates of installation. The headband " AUCUNE DATA" visible
Upstairs is normal: a sensor had just been installed and was still heating.

## Windows app captures

`pc_app/01` et `pc_app/02` are real windows of `GlycemieConnexion.exe`, photographed on
The PC. The identifiers we see are **invented**: they come from `tools/pc_app/demo_capture.pyw`,
which fills the window with examples without opening any serial port.

## A single image mask

Demonstration mode does not affect consumables: the page CONSOMMABLES displayed therefore
the **real dates** of installation of the sensor and change of the catheter. These are
personal health information.

On `04_consommables.png`, they have been replaced by visible grey bands**:
date of installation, current day, remaining time, deadline, exceedance. The wording,
page, buttons and stocks are intact. Nothing was invented or replaced by a
False value: masked areas can be seen.

The other four images are not retouched.

## Re-catching

```powershell
python tools\serial_capture.py --port COM7 --duration 190 `
  --send "2:demo normal" `
  --send "6:page 0"  --send "10:snap" `
  --send "36:page 1" --send "40:snap" `
  --send "66:page 2" --send "70:snap" `
  --send "96:page 3" --send "100:snap" `
  --send "126:page 4" --send "130:snap" `
  --send "158:demo off" --send "162:page 0" `
  --log capture.log --png page.png
```

Un `snap` takes about ten seconds to be transmitted: leave at least 25 seconds in between.
`demo off` at the end is important: it makes the hand to the real data.
